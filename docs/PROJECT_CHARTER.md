## Future Product Direction

WatchLog's future roadmap extends the existing offline-first personal media tracker without weakening its core ownership and local-data principles.

Future capabilities are organized into bounded workstreams so that each expansion can be independently discovered, architected, authorized, and validated. The roadmap explicitly separates local intelligence, release experiences, data portability, availability, complex tracking, external tracking, synchronization, people/spoilers, optional AI, cross-media support, and private social capabilities.

The following product principles remain in force:

- Core tracking remains usable without cloud services.
- Backup/recovery remains distinct from synchronization.
- Streaming availability remains distinct from scrobbling.
- AI remains optional rather than a core dependency.
- New media domains require dedicated domain analysis.
- Social/account capabilities require explicit privacy and backend architecture decisions.

The detailed future feature inventory is maintained in `docs/future-enhhancements.md`; architectural constraints are maintained in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; sequencing belongs in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
